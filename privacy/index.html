Atlas Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2026

Atlas is an iPhone workout tracker for logging strength training, reviewing workout history, and optionally recording heart-rate samples from compatible Bluetooth heart-rate sensors during workouts.

Summary

Atlas is designed to keep your training data under your control. The current public build does not require an account, does not use ads, does not use analytics SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not send your workout data to a developer-operated server.

Data Stored On Your Device

Atlas may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Workout sessions, exercises, sets, weights, reps, set types, rest timing, and workout notes.
  • Training preferences such as body weight, muscle-volume targets, and exercise credit rules.
  • Optional heart-rate samples captured from compatible Bluetooth heart-rate sensors while you use the sensor feature during a workout.
  • Export and restore preferences, such as whether automatic export is enabled and the user-selected folder bookmark used by iOS.

This data is used to provide Atlas features such as workout history, progress review, muscle-volume analysis, training math customization, export, restore, and workout heart-rate analytics.

Bluetooth Heart-Rate Sensors

Atlas can connect to compatible Bluetooth heart-rate sensors when you choose to start the heart-rate sensor feature. Heart-rate samples captured during a workout are stored locally with the workout so you can review intensity and detailed analytics later.

Bluetooth sensor use is optional. You can use the core workout logging features without connecting a heart-rate sensor.

Apple Health

The current public build of Atlas does not use HealthKit and does not request access to Apple Health.

Export And Restore

Atlas includes manual export and restore tools so you can keep a copy of your workout data. If you choose to export data, iOS presents sharing or document-picker controls and you decide where the files go.

Atlas may also mirror exports to a folder you choose through the iOS document picker if you enable automatic export. That folder is selected and controlled by you.

Data Collection

The current public build of Atlas does not collect data for the developer. Atlas does not operate a backend service and does not transmit your workout data, heart-rate samples, or preferences to a developer-operated server.

Tracking, Advertising, And Analytics

Atlas does not track you across apps or websites. Atlas does not include third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or data broker integrations.

Data Sharing

Atlas does not sell your data. Atlas does not share your data with advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers.

If you choose to export or share your data using iOS system features, the destination and handling of that exported data are controlled by you and the services or apps you choose.

Children

Atlas is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Medical Disclaimer

Atlas is for fitness tracking and training review only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified medical professional before making medical decisions.

Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated if Atlas changes how it handles data. If data practices change, the App Store privacy information and this policy should be updated accordingly.
