Atlas Privacy Policy

Updated November 8, 2026 — local-only release

Current local-only app

Atlas stores workouts, exercise information, weights, reps, set kinds, notes, training preferences and custom exercises locally on your device. It uses these records for logging, workout history and training analysis. No account is required. The current local-only app has no backend connection, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK.

Atlas does not collect or transmit your workout data to an Atlas server. You control manual export destinations using Apple's share sheet or document picker. Automatic exports, if enabled, write to the folder you select; that folder may use your chosen cloud storage provider. Those providers handle exported files under their own policies.

Live Activities display workout information on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. They use local app updates and do not use a remote push service.

Storage and deletion

Local records remain on the device until you delete them or remove the app and its data. Keep an export before removing the app. Workout exports cover workout history, not all preferences or the separate custom exercise catalog. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Earlier Social beta versions

Earlier beta versions offered optional cloud accounts and workout sharing. Those features are absent from the local-only release. Removing them from the app does not establish that previously collected cloud data or provider backups were deleted. For questions about data supplied to an earlier beta, contact support. Do not post private workout or account information in a public support issue.

Permissions and contact

The current app does not request Apple Health, location or contacts access. Atlas is a fitness tracker, not a medical service.
