Atlas Community Standards

Historical reference for earlier Social beta versions. The current local-only Atlas app has no Social features. Contact support about earlier beta data; the instructions below apply only to those earlier versions.

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Atlas Social is for supportive strength-training accountability and healthy competition. Everyone who uses Social must be at least 13 and follow these rules.

Be Respectful

Do not harass, threaten, shame, bully, impersonate, or target another person. Do not use a profile, Team, goal, or exercise name to attack someone or promote hatred toward a protected group.

Keep It Safe

Do not post sexual content, graphic violence, instructions for self-harm, credible threats, illegal content, dangerous challenges, spam, scams, or links intended to evade Atlas's safety controls.

Respect Privacy

Share only lifting information you have the right to share. Do not expose another person's private information or try to bypass workout, connection, Team, or blocking boundaries.

Compete Constructively

Leaderboards measure shared consistency, working sets, or workload volume. They are not a universal measure of strength, worth, health, or effort. Encourage teammates and avoid humiliating comparisons, coercive training, or unsafe pressure.

Reporting And Enforcement

Use the in-app Report action on a shared workout or athlete page. Use Block when you need immediate separation. Reports may be reviewed, retained for safety handling, and acted on through content removal, feature restriction, account suspension, or account removal when appropriate.

For urgent safety, privacy, or support concerns, use the Atlas support page. Do not include sensitive personal or workout information in a public issue.
