Atlas

Atlas Support

Atlas is an iPhone workout tracker for logging strength training, reviewing workout history, and optionally recording heart-rate samples from compatible Bluetooth heart-rate sensors during workouts.

Get Help

For support, questions, bug reports, or privacy questions, open a support request here:

https://github.com/austinlansing12-blip/atlas-support/issues

Please avoid including private workout, health, or personal details in a public issue.

Common Notes

  • Atlas does not require an account or login.
  • Workout data is stored locally on your device unless you choose to export it using iOS sharing or document-picker features.
  • Bluetooth heart-rate sensor support is optional. If a compatible sensor is not available, workout logging, history, analytics, export, and restore still work.
  • Atlas is for fitness tracking and training review only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
