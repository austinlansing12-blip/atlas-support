Atlas is an account-free iPhone strength-training tracker. Train, Reflect, workout history, export and restore work locally.

Support requests: Atlas support requests

Protect your information

Do not include private workout, health, contact or account details in a public issue.

Common help

Keep the installed app when updating; deleting it can remove its local workout records.

Use Settings to export workout history before moving or reinstalling. This is not a complete backup of your custom exercise catalog or calculation preferences.

Live Activities appear during active workouts when allowed by iOS. Dynamic Island controls require a compatible iPhone; iOS controls which activity is displayed.

The current app has no Social sign-in or backend. For questions about cloud data supplied to earlier beta versions, contact support without publishing private data.

Atlas does not request Apple Health access.
