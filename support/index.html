Atlas

Atlas Support

Atlas is an iPhone strength-training tracker with an optional Social surface for connections, Teams, shared lifting activity, goals, and healthy competition.

Get Help

For support, questions, bug reports, privacy requests, or safety concerns, open a support request here:

https://github.com/austinlansing12-blip/atlas-support/issues

Please avoid including private workout, health, or personal details in a public issue.

Common Notes

  • Atlas does not require an account or login for Train, Reflect, History, Settings, export, or restore.
  • Atlas Social is optional. A Social account mirrors only the workouts and detail you choose to share.
  • Workout notes, body mass, heart-rate data, Health data, and device metadata remain outside Social.
  • Bluetooth heart-rate sensor support is optional. If a compatible sensor is not available, workout logging, history, analytics, export, and restore still work.
  • Atlas is for fitness tracking and training review only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Social Safety And Privacy

  • Report a shared workout from its menu, or report an account from its athlete page.
  • Block an account to hide both people's activity from each other, including shared-Team activity.
  • Change a workout's audience or detail from its Social workout page.
  • Delete a cloud account at Social → Manage Social privacy & account → Delete cloud account. The independent workout archive on the iPhone is intentionally retained and can be managed separately.
